How it works

Fossil power displaced (hourly dispatch on a PVGIS solar profile) → tonnes avoided per pollutant (EMEP/EEA guidebook; US EPA AP-42 for generators) → damage avoided (EEA values in 2021 euros; Defra 2025 values in pounds for the UK). Full method and sources
